跨学科建造者 · A cross-disciplinary builder

I am a master's student in Financial Media at Peking University, with an undergraduate background in Internet of Things Engineering. My work sits at the intersection of LLM applications and evaluation, human-centered AI, social simulation, educational technology, and data-informed product systems.

Before returning to graduate study, I worked across product management and quality engineering at Veeva Systems, and later supported production management operations at Tencent IEG Global. These experiences shaped my interest in turning complex human and organizational problems into measurable, testable systems.
